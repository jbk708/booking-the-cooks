# Curry Powder Roast Cauliflower

> From Deidre

## Ingredients

 - Salt
 - Olive or sunflower oil
 - Cracked black pepper
 - 3-4g curry powder
 - Red pepper flakes to taste
 - 1 medium head cauliflower (or, more realistically, as much as you can fit on your toaster oven's baking sheet)
## Instructions
 - Chop florettes roughly. Don't worry about any small bits that fall off and instead keep them to throw in all together.
 - Preheat toaster oven to 220°C
 - Combine salt, pepper, curry powder, and red pepper flakes in a bowl and then add oil to form a thin paste
 - Use hands to work oil and powder mix into florettes until all are well coated and the heads are well seasoned
 - Evenly distribute the seasoned cauliflower on toaster oven baking sheet
 - Cook until golden/lightly charred
