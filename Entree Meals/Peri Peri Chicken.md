



# Peri Peri Chicken

> Based on the Cook's Illustrated Recipe image found [here](https://github.com/jbk708/booking-the-cooks/blob/main/Entree%20Meals/Peri%20Peri%20Chicken%20Ingredients%20Cropped%20%28Cooks%20Illustrated%29.png)


## Ingredients

- 4-10 chile de arbol, stemmed and (optionally) having the seeds removed
- 45 ml olive oil
- 30-40g salt
- 8-14 garlic cloves, peeled
	- Roughly chop the cloves if your blender cannot handle whole cloves
- 30ml tomato paste
- 1 large shallot, chopped
- 13 grams sugar
- 8g paprika
- 7g five spice powder
- 60ml lemon juice
- 3.5g lemon zest
- 2g cayenne pepper
- 3g ground black pepper
- 3 bay leaves, crushed
- 2.7kg bone in chicken thighs

## Instructions

 - Place all ingredients (besides the chicken of course) in a blender and blitz until the ingredients are well mixed into a reasonably consistent paste. 
	 - If you are having trouble getting the mix to blend, add water or oil along with some extra shallot to give the blender's blades more to bite in to.
 - Apply the paste to the dressed chicken, massaging it into the pieces, and then add the rest of the marinade to a container with the chicken.
	 - N.B. Be careful of touching any mucus membranes (not just your) after this step until you thoroughly clean your hands with dish-soap. The spice mix can be much hotter than the cooked chicken turns out to be.
 - Refrigerate for a minimum of 24 hours to let the marinade penetrate the chicken
 - When you are ready to start cooking, preheat the oven to 205°C  and get a large, heavy, oven-safe, pan (cast iron is best but a decent dutch oven can also work) to around 220°C (essentially the smoke point of avocado oil) on a large stove burner.
 - Oil the pan then immediately place the chicken thighs skin side down in to the pan as the oil starts to smoke along with the rest of the marinade
	 -  This often kicks up a decent amount of spicy smoke and spray, running the range fan and even an air purifier is advisable
 - Brown both sides of the chicken paying special attention to get the skin crispy and the sugar in the marinade caramelized on the skin side
 - Place the pan in the oven and let cook for one hour
 - Remove and serve over rice or quinoa (or a similar sort of carb I suppose) along with lemon wedges and your choice of vegetables
	 - I find well spiced and roasted Brussel sprouts to go particularly well with this dish but there are many good options including asparagus.
