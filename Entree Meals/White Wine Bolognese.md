# White Wine Bolognese

Ingredients

-   450g ground beef
    
-   450g pound hot Italian sausage
    
-   1 kilo mirepoix (equal parts onion, celery, and carrots)
    
-   2 large shallots or 3 normal shallots
    
	-   (optional) 1-3 cloves garlic (just a hint – really don’t go overboard here. The dish doesn’t need it and you could make garlic bread with that instead. You can also substitute a dash of garlic powder)
    
-   200g of parmesan
    

	-   N.B. Get a piece with a decent (doesn’t have to be massive) rind on it though. This is where most of the variation in weight comes from
    

		-   I’ve used a Gruyere
    

-   Butter
    
-   Olive Oil
    
-   10g Fennel Seeds
    

	-   This depends on the sausage you are using so experiment and err on the side of not enough fennel
    

-   1 part Coriander
    
-   5 parts Smoked Paprika
    
-   (optional) Cayenne
    
-   2 parts Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
    
-   1 part Marjoram (optional/not critical)
    
-   Good amount Cracked Black Pepper (important to cook in the fat with the veggies and to finish the dish at the end before serving)
    
-   Beurre manié
    

	-   (flour and butter whisked and kneaded together into paste)
    

  

Instructions

1.  Dice onions, shallots, bell pepper(s) and combine with mirepoix mix in large bowl
    

1.  (Optional) press/shave the garlic cloves in here
    

3.  De-case sausages and combine with ground beef, spices, and worcester sauce in large bowl.
    
4.  Place combined meat in heated dutch oven on high heat (olive oil in first and you can add cracked black pepper/salt) and break up the combined mass into small chunks while browning the meat.
    

1.  If the meat has a lot of water to express, simply allow it to boil off on high heat with the meat mostly pressed to the sides of the dutch oven
    

6.  Once meat is well browned and broken up into small pieces, remove it from the dutch oven to another bowl and place to the side
    
7.  Add combined vegetables to dutch oven with additional olive oil in as needed and cook until alliums are caramelized
    

1.  Add some butter while the veggies cook early on in replacement of most of the normal olive oil
    

9.  Remove cooked veggie mix to a large bowl and set aside
    
10.  Pour in bottle of white wine (minimum 450 ml) first into the dutch oven after giving it a minute or two to heat up even more so that the wine quickly dissolves the fond. Then add the chicken stock and the parmesan rind. Briefly bring up to a boil and then reduce to simmer until stock is heavily reduced
    

1.  Lid on or off – you for sure know yourself
    

12.  Begin to cook the pasta so that it finishes with enough time to briefly settle before being combined with everything
    
13.  (Optional) When reduction is almost complete (circa 20 minutes left) add in a few (emphasis on just a few) drops of crab sauce and make sure to stir it in
    
14.  Remove parmesan rind from stock and then whisk the beurre manié ( should need around 2 tbsp) into stock to until sufficiently thickened to lightly coat pasta.
    

1.  In my experience this also deepens the brown color of the stock which can be something to look for when knowing if you’ve done enough
    

16.  Combine the pasta, meat, and veggies in the dutch oven with the finished sauce
    
17.  Serve with freshly grated parmesan and dusting of cracked black pepper
    

  
  

## Vegetarian Bolognese Notes

Replaced meats 1 to 1 with impossible beef and soy chorizo with some slight increases/adjustments in seasoning to compensate.

Added a tiny amount of lemon juice while cooking veggies and after they expressed water to attempt to add brightness/mitigate the heaviness of the dish. Not noticeably lemony in the final outcome which is good
