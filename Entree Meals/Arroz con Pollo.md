

# Curry Powder Roast Cauliflower

> From Robert (vaguely)

## Ingredients

 - 200g bacon (minimum)
 - 1L chicken stock
 - 1.5kg bone in chicken thighs
 -  Olive oil
	 - For the initial browning of the chicken skin I often prefer to start with a higher flashpoint oil like avocado or sunflower
 - +10 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
 - 1.5 large yellow onions
 - 1 large red or orange bell pepper
 - 1 can of black, kidney, or pinto beans
 - 450-500g long grain white rice
 - 2 parts cumin
 - 1 part coriander
 - 3 parts paprika
 - Pinch of cayenne pepper
	 - Or you can grind up some chile de arbol or chile colorado to substitute and add a little more than you would for the cayenne
 - 1 part salt
 - 1 part oregano
 - 80 ml sofrito (very good but not entirely necessary)

## Instructions

 - Combine dry spices with a small amount of olive oil and massage into the dressed chicken thighs
 - Refrigerate and let marinate for at least 24 hours
 - Cook the bacon to your desired chewiness
 - Remove bacon and drain excess melted bacon fat
 - Place seasoned chicken skin side down in the pan and brown both sides on high heat. Roughly 4 minutes a side
 - Remove the chicken
 - Add aromatics and then the bell pepper after the aromatics begin to get translucent. Medium heat throughout
 - Add sofrito when the mixture above is almost done
 - Remove the mixture
	 - Optionally deglaze here with a small amount of neutral spirit or not too acidic white wine
 - Add rice and slightly brown it in the fond and oil
 - Add chicken stock and bring rice and stock to a simmer
 - Add the veggies after reaching a simmer
 - Add the chicken halfway through the rice's cook time
 - Cover and let the rice slowly absorb the flavor and liquid on a low simmer
 - Cook the beans in a separate pan and then add them to the main dish when the rice has only a few minutes left
 - Allow the rice to finish absorbing the rice and then mix everything together
 - Serve
