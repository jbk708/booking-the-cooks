# 5 Minute Baguette

## Ingredients

ingredient | quantity | note
-|-|-
Flour | 950 g | All purpose is fine
Salt | ~16 g | 
Yeast | .75 tsp | 
Water | 730 ml | lukewarm

## Steps
1. Mix all ingredients for 2 minutes with ~~the back of a spoon~~ **your hands**.
2. Cover bowl overnight – rest 8-10 hours.
3. Sprinkle flour on dough, bench, and hands.
4. cut and strech ***gently*** into 3 or 4 loafs.
5. bake at 250°C or 480°F for about 25 minutes
6. let it chill before cutting
